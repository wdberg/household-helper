Install Household Helper

Adds it to your phone like any other app.
iPhone
  1. Open this link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Android
  1. Open this link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add.

Once installed, always open it from the app icon. Everything you change is saved on this phone. To share it with your family, open the Family & sign in tab; it explains each step. On iPhone the installed app keeps its data separate from Safari, so sign in inside the installed app.

Family & sign in

How to set up your family and sign in

Step-by-step instructions
  1. One adult starts the family. On this tab, tap Start a new family. Enter your name, your family's name, your email and a password. You become the family's owner.
  2. Add everyone who lives in the house. Under Everyone in the house, type each person's name and tap Add. Include little ones who won't use the app. These names are used for who cooks, chores and shopping lists.
  3. Invite the people who'll use the app. Under Invite someone, choose Adult or Kid, tap Create invite code, then Share invite to send it by message. One code works for 7 days and for as many people as you like.
  4. They install the app and join. They open the link in the message on their phone, add the app to their home screen (iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen; Android: Chrome → Install app), then go to Family & sign in → Join my family. The code is filled in for them; they add their name, email and a password.
  5. Set who cooks each night. Under Who cooks each night, pick the usual cook for each day. Change any single night on the Calendar.

Signing in on another phone or tablet

  1. Install the app on it the same way.
  2. Go to Family & sign in → Sign in, and use your own email and password.
  3. Everything appears straight away. Changes made on any phone show on the others within a few seconds.

Adults and kids

  • Owner (whoever started the family, plus anyone they make an owner): can do everything, including resetting passwords, changing roles and deleting the family.
  • Adults: can change everything and invite people.
  • Kids: can see everything, tick off chores (and earn stars) and add to shopping lists, but can't change plans, chores or settings.

Forgot your password?

  1. Ask the family's owner (or any adult, for kids) to open Family & sign in and tap Reset password next to your name.
  2. They give you the temporary password it shows.
  3. Sign in with your email and that password. The app asks you to choose your own straight away.

Owners: make another adult an owner too, so someone can reset your password if you forget it. Your family's plans and lists aren't affected by a reset.

Privacy notice

What's stored, where, and who can see it

What's stored

  • What your family adds to the app: meal plan changes, recipes, calendar events, chores, shopping lists, pantry items, prices and settings.
  • For each login: the person's name, email address and role (owner, adult or kid). Passwords are never stored as typed; they're kept scrambled (hashed) so no one can read them.

Where it's kept

  • With Supabase, a database service, in its Sydney, Australia data centre. Data travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and Supabase encrypts the data it stores.
  • A copy is also kept on each signed-in phone so the app works offline. Signing out removes it from that phone.

Who can see it

  • Only people signed in to your family. The database itself blocks every other family and anyone not signed in.
  • The person who runs this app can access the database to keep it working. It isn't end-to-end encrypted.
  • There are no ads or tracking, and your data is never sold or shared.

Other services the app uses

  • Google Fonts (for the app's lettering) and jsDelivr (for the barcode reader) are loaded from their servers, which see your phone's internet address.
  • Signing up and signing in use Cloudflare Turnstile, a privacy-friendly check that stops automated sign-ups. Cloudflare sees basic details of your browser to tell people from bots.
  • Tapping Find opens the supermarket's own website or app, with its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Any adult can remove someone from the family. Anyone can delete their own login under Your login.
  • The family's owner can delete the whole family: everything it has saved and every login in it are permanently deleted.
  • Use Back up & restore at the bottom of the page to keep your own copy.

Cheapest way to order

Based on your roster: one order each home week, plus one for home each away week, about 52 orders a year.

OptionFeeDelivery per year
Recommended: Woolworths Delivery Unlimited, annual plan.
  • At about 52 orders a year it costs $119, against roughly $364 paying per order at Coles or $572 at Woolworths. Join before 2 December to get the half-price $74.50 offer if it's still running.
  • Keep every order at $75 or more for $0 delivery. If a week comes in under, add pantry items or merge it with the next week.
  • Book Monday to Saturday slots; Sundays and public holidays add $2.
  • On swing, place the home order from work for delivery at home.
  • Most recipes here come from Woolworths, so the ingredients match their range.

Fees checked 19 Nov 2026 from Woolworths, CHOICE and ShopBack. Check them at checkout, as they change often.
